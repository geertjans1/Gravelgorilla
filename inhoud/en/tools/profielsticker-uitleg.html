Kilometre 87. Your legs say stop, your head wonders how long that climb still goes on, and somewhere in your back pocket is a bar you forgot about. Sound familiar? That's exactly what the profile sticker is for. One strip on your top tube or handlebar, and you see at a glance what's still to come.

What's on your sticker?

  • The elevation profile of your route, with the climbs marked. No more surprises after that one corner.
  • The surface: tarmac, gravel or sand as a coloured bar. The tool fetches it automatically from OpenStreetMap, or you draw it yourself.
  • Your stops: rest stops, water points, checkpoints or your place to sleep, with your expected arrival time.
  • Your power per section, so you don't burn all your matches on the first climb.
  • When to eat and drink. The tool calculates how many carbohydrates you need on the way and puts the moments to eat right on the sticker.

How it works

Drop in your GPX or TCX file, from Komoot, Strava, RideWithGPS, Garmin or the event website. Your sticker appears straight away, with default settings. Want it more precise? Then enter your weight, your FTP or level, your luggage and your eating habits. Choose the format, check the preview and print or download it.

Your route is not uploaded: the tool reads the file on your own device. Only if you let it fetch the surface are the coordinates of your route (without name or times) sent to a public OpenStreetMap server.

Ideal for

A long day ride through the Ardennes, your first gravel race or an ultra where you can no longer do clear maths at three in the morning. No GPX at hand? Try the sample ride in the tool.

All times, power figures and nutrition advice are estimates. Wind, groups and how you feel that day can make a big difference, and nutrition advice is not medical advice.
