Where can I ride gravel in the Netherlands?

In every province. Signposted gravel routes are mainly found in Limburg, the Achterhoek, Drenthe and Zeeland: often the gravel versions of the official mountain bike routes. GPX routes run from Friesland to Zeelandic Flanders, including the Veluwe, the Utrechtse Heuvelrug, Twente and Brabant. For a long tour there's the Green Divide (300 km from Naarden-Bussum to Zwolle) and the long station-to-station routes of Gravelrides.cc, such as Groningen – Maastricht.

Where can I ride gravel in Belgium?

Flanders has many gravel routes with GPX, including in the Kempen, Haspengouw, the Voerstreek, the Flemish Ardennes, the Pajottenland, the Hageland, the Heuvelland and on the coast. Toerisme Vlaams-Brabant has official routes along the 2024 Gravel World Championships course, and the Noorderkempen has its own gravel network with four routes of 66 to 101 km. Wallonia has only two routes in the list so far (Bastogne and Eupen); we are still looking for reliable, free sources there.

Are all gravel routes free?

Most are: the routes of mtbroutes.nl, Sporza, Toerisme Vlaams-Brabant and Drenthe.nl can be downloaded or followed for free (sometimes you pay a small contribution for maintenance). On Gravelrides.cc most routes are for paying members only. They have an orange "Members only" label in the list. Set the Cost filter to "Free only" to hide them.

Can I ride my gravel bike on any unpaved path?

No. Many forest and heath paths are for walkers only, and on some sandy tracks cycling is not allowed. On official routes you're fine: cycling is allowed there. Also watch the signs of the land manager, because some paths close in the breeding season or in wet weather.

Do I need a permit for a gravel route?

Sometimes. For some Dutch mountain bike and gravel routes you pay a small contribution for maintenance, with a vignet or a day ticket. Whether that applies to a route is stated on that route's source page.

What is the best gravel route for beginners?

Start with a signposted route of 20 to 40 km in a flat area, such as Ruinen in the Dwingelderveld (21 km) or Neede in the Achterhoek (27 km). Then you don't need to look at a screen and the distance is very doable. Set the distance in the finder to "Up to 30 km" or "30 – 60 km" for more options.
