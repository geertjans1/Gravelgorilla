3 All your gears in a row

Each dot is a gear: how fast you go at your chosen cadence.

The steepest climb is an estimate of what you can hold seated at your chosen cadence. We calculate with your weight, your power, your cadence in the lowest gear, rolling resistance on gravel and a bit of air resistance. Development = how many metres your bike moves forward per pedal revolution. For the cassettes the largest and smallest sprockets are exact; the sprockets in between are approximate.
