In the Netherlands and Belgium.

You follow signposted routes via posts or arrows. For a GPX route you download the file at the source and put it on your bike computer or phone.

Note: stay on the trails of the route, and check the source page whether you need a permit (vignet) or route contribution. Seasonal rules sometimes apply in nature areas. On Gravelrides.cc most routes are for members only: you can tell them by the orange label.

3 The routes

    Sources (checked in October 2026): the official gravel routes on mtbroutes.nl, Drenthe.nl, the Green Divide on Komoot, the Sporza Onverhard routes in Flanders, the official gravel routes of Toerisme Vlaams-Brabant on Fietsknooppunt.be (NodeMapp) and the routes of Gravelrides.cc (most for paying members only). The source pages are mostly in Dutch. Wallonia will follow as soon as we have found more reliable routes. Know a great gravel route with an official source? Tell the gorilla.
