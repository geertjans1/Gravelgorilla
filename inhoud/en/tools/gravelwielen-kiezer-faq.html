Carbon or aluminium gravel wheels: which is better?

Carbon wheels are usually lighter and stiffer, and feel faster when accelerating and climbing, but they are more expensive. Aluminium wheels are more affordable and forgiving when you hit a stone or tree root. For many gravel riders, a good aluminium wheelset is the best value for money.

700c or 650b for gravel?

700c is the standard on most gravel bikes and rolls easily over bumps. 650b is a smaller wheel that takes a wider tyre; that gives more volume and comfort on rough terrain. Do check whether your frame and fork suit the size and tyre width you want.

Which internal rim width suits my gravel tyres?

For gravel tyres of about 38 to 50 mm, an internal width of around 21 to 25 mm is common. A wider rim gives the tyre a fuller shape and more support at low pressure. Always check the size chart of the tyre brand.
