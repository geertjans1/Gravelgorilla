All gravel tours in the Netherlands, Belgium and just across the border.

A gravel tour is not a race: you sign up, ride at your own pace and there's usually a coffee stop along the way.

Check the organiser's website just before the day: rides are sometimes moved or cancelled, for example in a storm or when it's too wet.

4 The rides

Sources (checked in October 2026): the touring calendar of the NTFU (fietssport.nl), the recreational calendar of Cycling Vlaanderen and the calendar of the FFBC in Wallonia, plus rides we found via NL Tour Rides, Land van Grind en Zand and Grit and then checked with the organiser. Each ride links to its official page (mostly in Dutch or French). Rides that are over disappear from the list automatically.

Do you organise a gravel tour yourself?

Submit it and we'll add it to the calendar for free. We check every submission first, so it may take a few days before your ride appears.
