What is a profile sticker?

A narrow strip for your top tube, handlebar or stem with the elevation profile of your route. You see at a glance where the climbs are, what the surface is, where your stops are, what power to hold per section and when to eat and drink.

Which route files can I use?

GPX and TCX files, for example from Komoot, Strava, RideWithGPS, Garmin or an event website. No file at hand? Try the sample ride in the tool.

Is my route uploaded?

No. The tool reads your file on your own device. Only if you let it fetch the surface are the coordinates of your route, without name or times, sent to OpenStreetMap to see where there is tarmac, gravel or sand.

Which formats can I make?

A strip for your top tube (200 × 30 mm), your handlebar (130 × 24 mm) or your stem (90 × 32 mm), a cheat sheet (35 × 110 mm) or a custom size. You can print the sticker on A4 or download it as PNG or SVG.

Can I add and move stops?

Yes. Tap the profile to place a stop, such as a rest stop, water point or place to sleep. Want to move it? Drag the numbered circle left or right. Arrival times, water and food are recalculated straight away.
