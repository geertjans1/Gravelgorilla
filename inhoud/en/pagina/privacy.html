Privacy statement

In short: no cookies, no ads and no account. Below you can read exactly what the site does use.

Who is responsible?

Gravel Gorilla (gravelgorilla.nl) is made by Geert Jans in the Netherlands. Questions about privacy? Email {{MAIL}}.

No cookies, no tracking

The site does not set cookies, shows no ads and uses no programs that follow you around the internet (such as Google Analytics or a Facebook pixel). You don't need an account and you never have to enter your name or email address.

What the site does use

Usage counter of the tools

Each tool shows how often it has been used. When you open a tool page, the counter of that tool goes up by one. Nothing about you is stored: no IP address, no cookie, only the total per tool. We also count per tool how often someone starts using it (fills something in or clicks something) and how often someone clicks through to a next tool. These are also only totals, visible only to the site owner.

Voting on the tools

You can give a tool an upvote or a downvote. Only the total number of votes per tool is stored. Which vote you gave is remembered only by your own browser, so you can change or withdraw it.

Settings on your own device

Your choice of light or dark mode, your ticks in the packing list and your settings in the profile sticker (such as your weight and food preferences) are stored in your own browser. That data does not reach us. You remove it by deleting the site data in your browser.

Weather forecast in the clothing advice

If you click "Get weather", the place name and date you entered are sent to Open-Meteo, a free weather service, to fetch the forecast. If you don't, nothing is sent.

Surface data in the profile sticker

Your route file (GPX or TCX) is read on your own device and not uploaded. If you let the tool fetch the surface, only the coordinates of the route, without name or times, are sent via our server to the map servers of OpenStreetMap. Our server remembers OpenStreetMap's answer (which roads lie along that route) for 30 days, so the same route loads faster next time. Who the route belongs to is not stored.

Fonts and product photos

The site's fonts are loaded via Google Fonts, and the finders load product photos from the websites of the bike brands. Your browser connects to those servers for this, which technically means they see your IP address.

Web server log files

As with almost every website, the server at our hosting provider Cloud86 keeps technical log files, including IP address, time and the requested page. They are only used for security and solving problems, and are deleted automatically after a limited time.

If you email us

If you email us, we only use your email address and message to reply to you. We don't share them with anyone.

Links to other websites

The tools link to websites of bike brands, shops and events. Their own privacy rules apply on those websites.

Your rights

You have the right to ask which data we have about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Because the site processes hardly any personal data, in practice this mainly concerns emails you have sent us. Send your request to {{MAIL}}. If you disagree with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or the supervisory authority in your own country.
