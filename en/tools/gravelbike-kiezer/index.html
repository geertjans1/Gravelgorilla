Five short questions, instant results. For every bike I calculate an honest Match Score, with the reasons — and afterwards you can optionally refine your advice.

1 How much gravel experience do you have?
This decides how much say you get — and whether I steer you towards accessible or high-end specs.
2 What do you mainly want to do with your gravel bike?
This steers the geometry and comfort of the match.
3 What kind of bike do you ride now?
This tells me what will change for you — so I can tailor my advice to it.
4 How tall are you?
Maybe the most important question: I move bikes that don't come in your size down and put a ✓ badge on the bikes that do fit.
5 What is your budget?
The guide price of the complete bike. I leave out bikes above your budget; keenly priced bikes below it simply count too.
6 What matters most to you?
This helps me choose between Shimano/SRAM, mechanical or electronic and 1× or 2×.
7 What weighs most in your choice?
Expert mode: you decide what the Match Score gives extra weight to.
8 Where do you ride most?
This colours my advice and gives suitable bikes a small nudge — not a hard filter.

About this finder

Are you really sitting right on your gravel bike? I see it with too many riders on a group ride: they dangle at the back, not because their legs can't cope, but because the bike simply doesn't suit them.

Because not every gravel bike is the same. Geometry, tyre size, wheel size and your position on the bars together decide how a bike behaves once the tarmac ends and the gravel starts crunching under your tyres. If you mainly ride long tours over rolling hills, something very different works for you than if you head out bikepacking with loaded bags, or want to race hard at a gravel event. Ride the wrong bike and you feel it straight away: less grip in a corner, a back that starts complaining after two hours, or steering that gets nervous as soon as the sand gets loose.

That's why we built the gravel bike finder. Answer a few questions about how you ride, where you usually ride and what your budget is, and you get advice that truly suits your way of riding. No sales pitch, just an honest starting point so you don't have to look for a different bike again in six months.

Once you've found the right bike, it pays to look at a bike fit too. For most gravel riders that's no luxury. Gravel means long days in the saddle over uneven ground, and then small deviations in saddle height, reach or cleat position suddenly hurt after a few hours: a tingling hand, a stabbing knee or a back that stops cooperating after lunch. A good fitter adjusts the bike to your body instead of the other way round, and often finds more comfort and power in a bike that already seemed good enough.

There is a flip side. A proper bike fit easily costs between 150 and 300 euros and usually half a day of your time. A fit also doesn't fix the wrong frame size: if your bike is really too big or too small, a fitter can compensate, but it won't solve the core of the problem. And for someone who only rides an easy loop now and then, the benefit is simply smaller than for someone who spends hours on the bike every weekend. So we see it as a valuable next step, not the first one: choose the right bike first, then consider a fit to get the most out of it.

Frequently asked questions

How does the Gravel Bike Finder work?

You answer five short questions: how much experience you have, what you want to do with your bike, how tall you are, what your budget is and where you ride most. The finder gives every bike a Match Score and shows why a bike does or doesn't suit you. Then you can refine your advice, for example with the bike you ride now.

How many gravel bikes are in the finder?

147 models from 13 brands, all sold in the Netherlands, including 21 electric gravel bikes. The data was updated in June 2026.

How do I know which gravel bike size I need?

The finder asks your height and shows whether a model is available in your size. In doubt between two sizes? Look at the brand's geometry chart (especially stack and reach), try the frame size finder and take a test ride if you can.

Can I compare gravel bikes?

Yes. In the tab "Compare all gravel bikes" you filter by price, frame, drivetrain, tyre width and more. You can also tick individual bikes and put them side by side.

Is the Gravel Bike Finder free?

Yes, the finder is free and you don't need an account. The Match Score is calculated only from your answers and the specifications of the bikes.
