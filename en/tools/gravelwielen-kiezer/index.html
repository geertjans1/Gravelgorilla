🛒 Where to buy gravel wheels? Tips & reliable web shops Expand +
Every wheelset has its own "View & order" button that leads to the official brand site or a reliable web shop. A few tips for buying separate gravel wheels:
• Specify your freehub. When ordering you choose the cassette body that suits your groupset (Shimano HG, SRAM XDR, Campagnolo N3W or Micro Spline). Not sure? See step 3 of "Find my wheelset".
• Watch out for hookless. Hookless rims only work with approved tubeless tyres and a lower maximum tyre pressure — check the manufacturer's tyre compatibility.
• Direct-to-consumer brands (such as Hunt, Scope, Winspace, Newmen) mainly sell through their own site; big brands (DT Swiss, Zipp, Shimano) also through bike shops and web shops like Mantel, Bike-Components, BIKE24 and Bike-Discount.

About this finder

Do you go for that super-light carbon wheelset, or the sturdy aluminium set that survives a knock against a tree root? With gravel wheels you quickly get lost in rim widths, spoke patterns and budgets pointing in every direction.

This finder makes that choice a lot simpler. Tell us where you usually ride, how much you want to spend and what matters most to you (speed, comfort or simply toughness that can take a beating), and within a minute you get advice that suits your bike and riding style. No technical jargon, no endless reviews to dig through. Just a clear answer you can head out with.

Frequently asked questions

Carbon or aluminium gravel wheels: which is better?

Carbon wheels are usually lighter and stiffer, and feel faster when accelerating and climbing, but they are more expensive. Aluminium wheels are more affordable and forgiving when you hit a stone or tree root. For many gravel riders, a good aluminium wheelset is the best value for money.

700c or 650b for gravel?

700c is the standard on most gravel bikes and rolls easily over bumps. 650b is a smaller wheel that takes a wider tyre; that gives more volume and comfort on rough terrain. Do check whether your frame and fork suit the size and tyre width you want.

Which internal rim width suits my gravel tyres?

For gravel tyres of about 38 to 50 mm, an internal width of around 21 to 25 mm is common. A wider rim gives the tyre a fuller shape and more support at low pressure. Always check the size chart of the tyre brand.

How many wheelsets are in the finder?

Over 100 gravel wheelsets from about 40 brands, from affordable aluminium to high-end carbon. Prices are approximate, as of June 2026.

Can I compare wheelsets side by side?

Yes. You can filter by budget, material, wheel size and use, and tick individual wheelsets to put them side by side.
