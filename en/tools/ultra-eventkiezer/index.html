About this finder

Twenty tabs open, three registration forms half filled in and still no idea which ultra you'll ride this year. Sound familiar? The range of ultra gravel events is growing fast, from coastal rides along the North Sea to bikepacking routes through the Alps, and somewhere among all those options you lose track.

This finder takes that doubt away. Answer a few questions about your experience, your fitness, the terrain that gives you energy and how many days you have, and you get a shortlist of events that really suit you. No digging through lists, just a few targeted matches you can act on straight away.

Fill it in, let an event that wasn't on your radar yet surprise you, and book your spot before it's full.

Frequently asked questions

What is an ultra gravel event?

An ultra is a long ride or race over hundreds to thousands of kilometres, often spread over several days. Many ultras go largely over gravel and unpaved roads and are ridden bikepacking style: with your luggage in bags on your bike.

What does self-supported mean?

At a self-supported event you arrange everything yourself: food, drink, sleep and repairs. There is no support car or feed station from the organisation. You may use shops, campsites and hotels that are open to everyone.

How many events are in the finder?

80 ultra and bikepacking events in 31 countries and six continents, for the 2027 and 2028 seasons. From about 460 km through Tuscany to over 7,000 km from the North Cape to Tarifa.

What can I filter on?

Month, continent, country, distance, surface (tarmac, mixed or unpaved) and type of bike. You can also search by name or place and sort by date, distance or elevation gain.

Are dates and distances always correct?

They are guide values from the organisers. Dates and routes can change, and some events are still provisional. So always check the event's official website before you register.
