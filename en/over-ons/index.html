About us

From frustration to twelve tools. Too many articles promised an answer and still left you guessing. So we build tools that do the maths and the choosing for you.

Who is behind it?

Hi, I'm Geert Jans

I ride gravel myself, and I make all the tools on this site. Gravel Gorilla started with a frustration: I was looking for answers to ordinary questions, like which gravel bike suits me, how much air goes in my tyres and what to wear at ten degrees with wind. Mostly I found general articles that still leave you guessing.

So I started researching, calculating and comparing myself. These tools are the result. They are free, you don't need an account and there are no ads on the site.

Spot a mistake, missing a brand or have an idea for a new tool? Email me at info@gravelgorilla.nl. I read everything. (I'm Dutch, so the site started in Dutch; English is very welcome.)

How we build a tool

  1. KM 01

    Digging

    Collecting specs, checking prices, ploughing through reviews. Every tool starts with a mountain of data that we check by hand.

  2. KM 02

    Building

    From that data we make a tool that answers one question well. Not twenty screens, but a few choices that really help you.

  3. KM 03

    Testing

    On phone and computer, every outcome checked. If something doesn't work well, it goes back to the workshop.

  4. KM 04

    Keeping up

    New models, new prices, new events: a tool is never finished. We update the data so the advice stays right.
